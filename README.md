## Hi, I'm Theodor

I'm finishing my BSc in Informatics at the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) in Lugano. I build full stack applications, mostly with Java and Spring Boot on the backend and React on the frontend.

During my studies I interned as a software developer at EOC, the hospital network of canton Ticino, working in a Scrum team on the new electronic patient record used by the region's hospitals.

I have also spent almost ten years working in hospitality, on both the operational and the administrative side. That is where most of my ideas come from for my work-in-progress hospitality software suite.

### Featured work

* **[Hotel staff scheduling, a case study](https://github.com/theodorvavassori/hotel-staff-scheduling-case-study)**
  My final bachelor project. Seven interviews with hotel managers, a constraint solver based scheduling prototype with AI powered data import, and a usability evaluation with real users.
* **[IKEA Hacks search engine](https://github.com/sccjrd/IR_Project)**
  An information retrieval system built with a colleague: tens of thousands of scraped pages, indexed and searchable.
